[b][color=#e69138][size=4]CURRENT RELEASE — PROGRESSION QOL 0.9.6 RC1[/size][/color][/b]

[b]Validated baseline:[/b] DragonSword 1.0.10, Steam build 24831799.

[b]New in 0.9.6 RC1[/b]

- Added an independent Tower of Trials multiplier from x1 to x10.
- Covers 68 verified Fragment of Memory rows and 10 Whirling Thoughts rows.
- Includes verified floor-clear and cumulative-star rewards.
- Added a dedicated Tower tab with clear reward and claim notes.
- Existing saved profiles remain compatible; Tower starts at vanilla x1 when an older profile is loaded.
- Added automatic identity and quantity checks for all 78 Tower reward rows during every build.

[b]Important Tower behavior[/b]

The selected multiplier applies only when an affected reward is newly earned or still waiting to be claimed. It does not grant rewards that were already collected, and it does not change stars or difficulty.

[b]DragonSword 1.0.11[/b]

1.0.11 is newer than our verified table baseline. The app displays a compatibility warning and allows you to continue after confirmation. Players have reported the prior version working, but we have not certified every 1.0.11 reward table yet. If anything behaves unexpectedly, restore vanilla settings and let us know the activity and expected versus received reward.

[b]Install[/b]

Extract the complete download, close the game, run DragonSword.ProgressionQoL.exe, choose your settings, and select Build + Install. The app replaces only its own PAK and preserves the previous copy as a disabled backup.
